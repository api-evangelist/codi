# Codi

> Codi is a managed flexible office-space provider (office-as-a-service) operating in San Francisco and New York City. It delivers fully-managed private offices for teams — bundling furniture, cleaning and restocking, tech and network management, vendor coordination, room booking, and an AI office assistant into a single turnkey monthly subscription. Codi is an a16z portfolio company. As of this profile it publishes no public developer API, SDKs, or API documentation; its digital surface is a members' web app and a hosted help center.
